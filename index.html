A finding is a starting point.

The useful question is what the evidence supports.

Argo brings language models into a structured source-code review. Human review checks the result; the upstream record shows what happened next. A merged fix, an open pull request and an unpatched advisory are different outcomes.

These records show practical work, rather than a complete measurement of what the system can find or miss. For the pipeline, tradeoffs and limitations, read the documentation.

Luigi is credited on LiveKit’s Security Hall of Fame. That recognition belongs to a private disclosure; the LiveKit records in this archive link to public fixes. The individual PRs do not name the reporter.
