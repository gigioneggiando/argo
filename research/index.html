A length field is not an allocation budget

MediaMTX trusted compact MoQ length fields as slice sizes before checking whether the message could contain that much data. A small unauthenticated input could trigger an unrecoverable allocation panic and stop the entire media server.

A valid signature is not a valid login

authentik verified a SAML assertion's signature and lifetime without proving it was meant for that SAML Source or tied to its login request. Because accepted assertions were not remembered, a valid assertion could also be replayed.

Panic recovery stays local

PocketBase recovered panics during ordinary request handling, but work moved into child goroutines lived beyond that boundary. A reachable panic could terminate the server instead of becoming a regular error.

Revocation must reach every live stream

ntfy removed a user's access or account, but its teardown helper stopped after cancelling the first matching subscription. Other live streams could continue receiving that topic's messages until they disconnected.

When identity data becomes policy syntax

Moquette inserted client IDs and usernames directly into pattern-based ACL rules. MQTT wildcard characters in those identities could broaden a tenant-scoped rule and grant cross-tenant read or write access.
